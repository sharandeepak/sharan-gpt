# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Senior tech recruiters and engineering hiring managers. They open the page in a browser tab between meetings, on a laptop, and have 30 to 90 seconds to decide whether Sharan is worth a deeper conversation. Most arrive from LinkedIn, an inbound email reply, or a referral message. They are skim‑readers, not deep‑readers. They distrust portfolio sites that look more polished than the work behind them.

## Product Purpose

A single page that proves Sharan Deepak R B is a senior backend engineer with measurable production impact, then lets the recruiter ask the questions they would normally email about. The resume is the credibility anchor; the assistant removes the friction of a follow‑up exchange. Success is a recruiter sending a real first message rather than bouncing.

## Brand Personality

Considered, exact, premium. Reads like a senior engineer wrote it, not a brand designer. Quiet confidence, no swagger. Voice is plain and verifiable: numbers when they exist, "I don't have enough verified information" when they don't. The interface should feel like a well‑made tool, not a campaign.

## Anti-references

- Generic dev portfolios with neon gradients, particle backgrounds, scroll‑hijack heroes.
- Glassmorphism dashboards and frosted "AI" cards.
- "I craft beautiful experiences" copy and self‑congratulatory taglines.
- Hero‑metric SaaS templates: big number, small label, three icon cards.
- Side‑stripe alert bars, decorative gradient text, identical icon‑card grids.
- Chatbots that animate floating orbs while typing.

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility before charm.** The resume is visible without scrolling. Every visual choice supports that or yields to it.
2. **The recruiter's questions, not Sharan's.** Suggested chips are written from HR's vocabulary ("impact at SurveySparrow"), not the candidate's ("my journey").
3. **Nothing the resume doesn't back.** The assistant cites only verified profile facts and admits gaps cleanly.
4. **Restraint as signal.** A senior engineer's tool looks calm. Density and rhythm carry the weight, not effects.
5. **Editorial copy.** Every label could appear in print. No "Let's get started," no "Awesome!", no exclamation marks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA on every theme. Text contrast verified per theme, not assumed.
- Full keyboard path: panel resize, theme switch, chat input focus (Cmd/Ctrl+K), resume download (Cmd/Ctrl+D), suggested chip activation.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes streaming carets, panel‑drag preview, and theme transition fades.
- Embedded PDF carries a visible "Download resume" affordance for users who can't read embedded PDFs (mobile Safari, screen readers).
- Live region announces streaming response start and completion, not every token.
